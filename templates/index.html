04 / ACCESSIBILITY

Science should be experienced by everyone.

When an image is analyzed, a plain-language description appears here. Use keyboard navigation and a screen reader to explore the controls.

Scientific note

This prototype analyzes image appearance, not the underlying physical measurements. Red does not automatically mean heat, and blue does not automatically mean cold: the original visualization legend and source data must be consulted before making scientific interpretations. The sound mappings are an artistic accessibility layer, not a substitute for the original data or its legend.
